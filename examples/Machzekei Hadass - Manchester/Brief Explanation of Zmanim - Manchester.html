A BRIEF EXPLANATION OF זמנים USED IN THIS LUACH

ALL זמנים ARE FOR MANCHESTER ONLY

1. עלות השחר א׳ - when the sun is 16.1° below the horizon. During the height of summer (20 May till 20 July) the sun does not reach that depth below the horizon hence the time given for עלות השחר is midnight.
2. עלות השחר ב׳ - when the sun is 12° below the horizon.
3. משיכיר - The earliest time for ברכת שמע and putting on טלית ותפילין, calculated as 15 mins. after עלות השחר ב׳.
4. משיכיר א׳ - We have added another calculation for winter months when the sun is 11° below the horizon.
5. הנץ החמה - when the upper edge of the sun disc is seen rising above the horizon at sea level.
6. סוף זמן קריאת שמע - the latest time for reciting קריאת שמע. Two times are given:
1) מג"א a quarter of the day between עלות השחר ב׳ and צאת הכוכבים
2) הגר"א a quarter of the day between sunrise and sunset.
7. סוף זמן תפלה - the latest time to finish Shacharis שמונה עשרה. Two times are given:
1) מג"א
2) גר"א
8. חצות - Midday. This is a precise astronomical calculation when the sun reaches its most southernly point. Midnight is 12 hours later but can vary by 1 minute each way.
9. מנחה גדולה - Earliest time to daven Mincha. It is calculated to be half an hour after חצות, שעות זמניות או זמניות, whichever is greater.
10. מנחה קטנה - times are given for the earliest time to daven מנחה קטנה calculated as 2 ½ hours זמניות before sunset.
11. פלג המנחה - one and a quarter שעות זמניות before the end of the day, 1) according to הגר"א calculated from עלות השחר until שקיעה, 2) according to מג"א ומ"ר calculated from עלות השחר until צאת הכוכבים.
12. זמן שבת - The latest time by when the נרות שבת should be lit and to refrain from doing Melocho. This is 15 mins. before שקיעה.
13. שקיעת החמה - Sunset when the sun has completely disappeared below the horizon at sea-level (the time printed in the calendar is one minute earlier).
14. צאת הכוכבים - The time for davening מעריב and reciting שמע and other Mitzvos which commence at nightfall. This is calculated when the sun is 7.08° below the horizon.
15. מוצאי שבת - when the sun is 8° below the horizon.
16. רבינו תם - This is 'nacht' calculated at minimum 72 minutes after שקיעה.
17. תענית - Start times are applicable when one stipulated before retiring for the night that one does not wish to accept the fast until the printed time (see O. C. 564).

General Rule

As a general rule, it is advisable to add one minute לחומרא to all times, as they will vary according to specific location and prevailing weather conditions.
